# Composition, media and behavior

Choose a composition appropriate to the offer. Compare plausible directions and, when selecting a new type identity, render several candidate fonts, usually three, beside the actual logo in headings, copy, navigation and numerals. Readability and compatibility matter more than unusual letterforms. Self-host licensed fonts when practical.

Give each page a visitor question and useful next action. Build only pages justified by real content. About should explain the company; portfolio and product pages need actual evidence. Keep centered introductions, reading columns, image scales and section density deliberate. Avoid repeating one card layout throughout the site.

Use semantic colors, a coherent spacing scale and responsive type. Design light/dark states independently when both are in scope. Confirm logo visibility, text and control contrast in each. Apply remembered themes before paint.

The user's default preferences avoid ornamental grids, blurred orbs, gradient text, fake terminals, floating annotations and status dots without a function. These are not universal prohibitions on legitimate product UI. Preserve explicit project choices and use decoration only when it belongs to the identity.

Viewport-scale sections, layered product stages, comparisons and continuous brand effects are options, not required ingredients. Select them because the content benefits. Use minimum heights and normal scrolling; let sections grow with enlarged text.

## Media

Integrate real captures or relevant licensed/original imagery into the composition. Choose scale and crops intentionally. Preserve intrinsic aspect ratios and reserve layout space. Use responsive assets and inspect transparency, backgrounds and every edge at changing widths.

When presenting product screenshots, avoid accidental padding, stretched images, opacity fading and pseudo-elements that look like malformed duplicate cards. If depth is useful, keep the screenshot readable through actual transformed frames. A single frame with a controlled shadow is often enough.

For before/after media, use authentic captures aligned in one coordinate system. A reveal must not independently rescale either side. Verify endpoints, intermediate positions, labels, pointer/keyboard controls, touch scrolling, replay and interruption. Do not invent responsive behavior in a capture of an old fixed-width site.

## Navigation and motion

Review header grouping, active state, logo size, target sizes, keyboard order, mobile overflow, closing behavior and focus return. Keep links for navigation and buttons for actions. Fixed UI must stay attached to the viewport throughout actual animated frames; inspect transformed ancestors and page snapshots when it moves unexpectedly.

Same-page fragment navigation should preserve native scrolling and history without unnecessarily invoking full-page snapshots. Page transitions must retain forward navigation, Back/Forward restoration, fragment destinations and focus. Provide unsupported-browser and reduced-motion paths.

Make disclosures smoothly reversible under rapid toggles and content changes. Preserve semantics, normal document flow and browser scrolling. Essential content must not depend on a reveal animation finishing.

Prefer motion that explains selection, state or spatial relationships. If an entrance settles into idle motion, inspect the handoff for velocity discontinuity, a pause or a restart. Check loop boundaries, clipping, stacking, resizing and interruption. Suspend expensive work offscreen and in hidden tabs; remove observers, listeners and animations on unmount.

Do not automatically attach play/pause buttons to small decorative branding. Prefer finite or subtle motion. When accessibility requires a stop mechanism, integrate it into the interface or choose a finite/still treatment. Reduced motion must give a complete usable result.

Substantial custom effects warrant an isolated tuning playground with the production renderer: meaningful controls/presets, replay, original comparison and validated settings transfer. Pause and zero speed must match their labels. Settings must not silently alter production defaults. Keep previews useful with mobile controls and enlarged text; verify comparison alignment at several split positions where a split comparison exists.

Use the simplest adequate renderer. Motion libraries, WebGL, canvas and rendered video need a specific benefit. A running timer is not evidence of visible movement.

## Customer copy and actions

Explain what the project does, who it helps, relevant evidence and how to act. Remove filler, invented authority and internal build narration. Put design rationale and implementation guidance in the reference, not company copy.

Use verified destinations. Expose real configured form behavior and honest pending/validation/failure states. An absent service configuration needs a working alternative or clear limitation, not a pretend success response. Preserve privacy facts and accessibility guidance; never turn an unfinished policy into an operative agreement.
