The fontsource builds drop `case`, `sups` and `ordn`; nothing in Phase 1
needs them (13.5). Loaded with `next/font/local`, `display: swap`, fallback
metrics from Times New Roman and Arial. The two roman files are preloaded
on every route; the Newsreader italic is a separate call with
`preload: false`, fetched only where the estimate sentence is set. The
Figtree italic (`figtree-latin-wght-italic.woff2`, 21 KB) was removed in
task J2b because no style uses it (the search and the measured effect are
in `TYPOGRAPHY.md` and `PERFORMANCE.md`).

## Icons

One project set drawn in G5 on a 24 px grid (`viewBox="0 0 24 24"`),
1.6 px stroke, round caps, `currentColor`, every icon paired with visible
text or an sr-only name. Rendered at 24 px in the rail, the tab bar and
the header toggles, and at 20 px inside buttons and chips.
Inline SVG in the component library; no icon font, no third-party set.

## Imagery rules

No stock photography, no generated art, no illustration in empty states.
Empty states and onboarding use text, the tide line and the mark. The
social card is the lockup on Mist with one line of copy. Photos in Phase 2
are the family's own, re-encoded server side.
