# Tidefern writing preferences

## Tells

- em-dash: ban
- negative-parallelism: ban
- significance-inflation: ban
- aphorism-closer: ban
- sycophantic-opener: ban
- chatbot-artifact: ban
- ai-vocabulary: ban
- rule-of-three: flag

## Style

- contractions: match-source
- register: warm, plain, calm
- emoji: ban

## Project rules

Tidefern speaks to a person about her body, her pregnancy or her child. Be
direct and gentle. Never diagnose, never alarm, never moralize. Predictions
are estimates and the copy says so. Loss, irregular cycles and missed days
are handled without judgment.

No em dashes in site copy, product copy, metadata, alt text, code comments,
commit messages or new project prose. Do not simulate them with en dashes or
double hyphens. Ordinary hyphenated words and technical syntax remain valid.

Public copy addresses the person using the product. Brand rationale, agent
instructions, implementation identifiers and build narration belong in the
`/design` reference or in internal documents, never on customer pages.

Health details never appear in URLs, page titles, notification text, email
subjects, log lines or analytics events. Route names stay neutral: `/today`,
`/journal`, `/family`, `/settings`.

## Never touch

- Verbatim quotations and third-party source files, including vendored skills.
- Code syntax, URLs, identifiers and the brand vector paths.
- Legal wording whose meaning requires specific language.

## Scope

These preferences come from the owner's explicit requests. They do not depend
on a voice profile. Do not read personal conversation archives to infer a voice.
